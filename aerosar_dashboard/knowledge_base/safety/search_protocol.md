# Search Protocol and Safety

Standard AEROSAR search protocols dictate a grid search pattern at an altitude of 30 meters for open terrain. 
Safety protocols require operators to monitor battery levels. 
If an incident is detected, the drone must hold position (loiter mode) while the incident is classified.
All protocols prioritize human safety. Before sending rescue personnel, ensure the area is free of immediate secondary hazards like fire, unstable structures, or chemical leaks based on drone telemetry and visual feed.
