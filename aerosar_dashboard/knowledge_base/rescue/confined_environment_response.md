# Confined Environment Rescue Response

When operating in a confined environment (such as collapsed structures, caves, or dense urban debris), AEROSAR procedures dictate caution.
The drone should maintain a safe standoff distance of at least 1.5 meters from physical obstacles. 
If a person is detected in a confined environment, standard response protocol involves marking the GPS coordinates and utilizing thermal/IR sensors to verify signs of life before deploying ground rescue teams.
Do not attempt close-proximity navigation without SLAM assurance.
